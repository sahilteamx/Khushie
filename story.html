The story behind the chapters

Our Story.

Bachpan mein jab hum mile the, tab hum dono ko kabhi pata nahi tha ki ek din hum yahan tak pahunch jayenge. 🥹

Woh baarish… aur usmein khelti-kudti hui woh ladki, jisko main bas ek jagah baithkar dekha karta tha. Mujhe kya pata tha ki ek din wahi ladki meri life ka itna important part ban jayegi. ❤️

Maine kabhi nahi socha tha ki Butterfly 🦋 Effect itna zyada mast hota hai. Kabhi ek dusre ke paas hokar bhi door hua karte the, aur aaj dekho… door hokar bhi ek dusre ke itne paas hain. 🤗

Aakhir kisne socha tha ki bachpan mein thoda sa gussa dilane par mujhe maar dene wali ladki aaj mere itne saare nakhre jhel rahi hain. 🤣😂

Hamari life mein bahot saare ups and downs aaye, lekin hum hamesha ek dusre ke saath rahe. Chahe problem kitni bhi badi kyun na ho, humne milkar solve ki, ek dusre ka saath diya aur ek dusre ko samjha.

Isi liye main bahot zyada lucky hoon ki aap meri ho… aur hamesha meri hi rahogi. 🫣❤️ Aur main toh hoon hi aapka. 🤗

Bas aap itna yaad rakhna — main aapke saath hoon. Jab tak hum ek saath hain, tab tak koi bhi problem aa jaaye, hum dono milkar dekh lenge. 🤗

Aur kabhi bhi aapko akela feel ho, ya accha na lage, toh bas ek message ya call 🤙🏻 kar dena. Main ek pal mein aapke paas aa jaunga. 😁❤️

Aur sabse funny baat toh ye hai ki humein pata hi nahi chala ki kab hamari dosti pyaar mein badal gayi. 🤣 Waise mujhe toh pata tha… bas aapko nahi pata tha. 😁

Shayad isi liye hamara bond thoda alag hai. Hum ek dusre se pyaar toh karte hi hain, lekin saath mein ek dusre ke best friends bhi hain. ❤️

Isliye jab bhi aapka mann kare, bas bol dena — “Mujhe mere best friend se baat karni hai.” 🤣

Aur usi pal… aapka best friend aapke saamne hoga. 🥰
